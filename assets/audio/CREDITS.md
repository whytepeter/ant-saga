# Sound credits

Every sound in this folder is made by `tools/process_sounds.py` from recordings by
**Joseph SARDIN, BigSoundBank.com**, released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain):
free for any use, commercial included, with no attribution required.
The author asks, but doesn't require, a credit: "Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com".

The original files live in `source/bigsoundbank/` (not in git); re-download them from
the links below to rebuild. The script cuts, pitches, loops and levels them.
