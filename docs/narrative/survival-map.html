In the game: the apple tree's missions (world/lawn/tree_missions.json)

Shown under the compass like the Wormways' (world/missions.gd), one step at a time.

The Old Tree

  1. Break sap off the tree's roots or at Sap Falls, for resin
  2. Make a resin torch

The Heartwood Stair

  1. Go into Root Hall
  2. Light your torch in the dark
  3. Find the way up inside the trunk

The Weavers' Ladder

  1. Find the silk ladder on the west side
  2. Climb it to the Old Bough
  3. Look up: something lives over the bough

The Orb Weaver

  1. Cut a guy line holding the web down
  2. Cut all three before it mends them
  3. Beat it on the bark, where it's clumsy
  4. Take the golden thread
