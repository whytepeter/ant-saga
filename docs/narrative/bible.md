## 1. Premise, and why

Two old warrior ants, **Opigo** and **Opumie**, set out from the kingdom under an ordinary back garden with the colony's one dose of a potion. It's meant for the hero of a prophecy: a two-legged saviour who will *stand tall where others crawl*. They pick the tall, confident bully.

- **The plan and the mix-up:** they climb the bully's trainer to tip the potion into his soda can so he'll drink it. He shakes the can, it bursts, the vial flies, and the potion and the fizz become a green mist. The mist stays low along the ground, so the only one who breathes it is the boy **crouching behind the boxes**: Amodu. The potion only works on giants, so the ants are safe. (The one who was hiding low got it: that's the theme, in the physics.)
- **The first moment of play:** the bully runs off screaming about "alien bugs", and his dropped can rolls past and misses them all. **Amodu does nothing about it: he has just been shrunk** and doesn't yet know what he can do. The first minutes are the shock of the new scale. His strength first shows in M2 (the wedged stone); the Know thread starts later, when he has time to think (the dew-drop fire at First Night).
- **Why shrink the saviour?** A giant can't stand among ants; he just steps on them. Opumie: *"A giant can't save us. A giant just steps on us. It has to be one of us, standing up."*
- **What Amodu wants:** to be big again. *"Can you make me big again?"* / *"Not us. The Queen might."* The Queen **can**, but **only after the war**, so his goal lives through the whole game. He follows the ants for his own reason first, and helping end the war comes as he starts to belong.
- **What the ants want:** a saviour to end the termite war. What they got is a mistake, at first.
- **Shape:** the game runs to about **Level 10**. Level 1 crosses the garden and ends shut out, going into Root Hall. Level 2 is the apple tree. Level 3 is the kingdom and the pond. The ending isn't fixed yet, and **Ugo is not the end of the story**.

## 2. Rules of the world

### The strength rule: space and grip, not weight
- Ants are strong: a worker carries 10–50× its weight, and a team can drag things far bigger than one ant. Two ants can't shift the camp stone; a team of about 30 could.
- **What limits anyone is how many bodies can get a grip.** On things that are round, smooth, wedged or in a tight space, only a few ants fit. Root Hall's door stone: *"Only four of us fit at it."*
- Amodu is **about the same size as an ant**; size gives neither side an edge. His edge is **a full-size boy's strength in one body**: in game he lifts things up to 2.6 m and pushes things up to 5.5 m. His tasks are the ones no team can do: where only a few ants fit, or that need lifting, throwing, swinging or leverage. Ants drag; Amodu lifts, throws and shoves.
- **Thin or flat things can be tipped or slid** even when they're bigger (the coin on its edge, the Root Hall slab).
- **Growing stronger is technique and tools,** not magic muscle: silk rope from Level 2 lets him rig and haul what he can't get his arms round.
- Hunger lowers what he can heave.

### What Amodu can do (from `world-facts.md`)
Walk, jog, sprint, crawl, and climb up, down and sideways. A **power jump** up to about 12 m high and 11 m forward (about 7× his height), a running leap of 6 m, and a shockwave when he lands from 4 m or more. He throws, **glides on a seed puff**, swims (slowly; see the exceptions), and has a **call** that reaches 35 m. From Level 2 he gains the **shout** (Ugo's echo riddle): sound shows him the shape of dark tunnels.
**He leaps; ants don't.** Ants climb and run but can't jump gaps, so leaps, throws and glides are his to use for them.

### Senses
Ants live mostly by smell and touch; for play, the companions can still point things out and use lamps like him. Termites are nearly blind and find you by vibration and scent, so standing still hides you. Humans can't hear a 5 mm voice.

### Deliberate exceptions (stated once, kept consistent)
1. Insects talk, have names, families (husbands, wives, grandmothers), jobs and jokes, live long, and use weapons and tools.
2. The potion works only on giants; it shrinks a human, keeps his body working at 5 mm, and leaves him a whole boy's strength (limited by space and grip).
3. **Story speed:** creatures move at a pace the player can play against.
4. The termites are an organised empire with an army, generals and a queen, and they fight the ants (in the real world, ants eat termites).
5. The termites keep **pill bugs** as livestock and drive them as battering rams (a mirror of the ants' aphid farming).
6. **One seed puff** carries the trio, never more.
7. **Swimming:** slow, tiring, no diving. Small puddles can still trap him if a scene needs it.
8. **Weaver ants** live in the apple tree, although this isn't the tropics.
9. **Glow-fungus lamps** give useful light (no heat).

## 3. Themes and tone

**Themes**
- **Worth isn't appearance.** Amodu is mocked in both worlds and earns respect by what he does, never by being "fixed".
- **The one who doesn't fit sees what the others can't** (the Know thread). The ants have myths about human junk; he knows what it is.
- **Legends adapt, legends die.** The musketeers are heroes past their prime, choosing to matter one more time.
- **Community against conquest.** The ants win by working together; the termite empire swallows everyone into sameness. Nobody wins by wiping the other side out.
- **Stand tall where others crawl:** literal (a two-legged boy among ants) and personal (a boy who stops hiding).

**Tone rules**
- Family-friendly, PEGI 12 at most. No sexual content, innuendo or seduction, anywhere.
- The comedy comes from character: Opigo's pride, Opumie's sarcasm, the ants' names for human things, and Amodu being funny about himself.
- Mockery of Amodu peaks early (Level 1 "mistake", Level 3 colony whispers) and turns into respect. Nobody gets a free cheap shot late on.
- Simple beats stay simple. He needed a weapon, so the ants gave him one. Props don't need backstory.
- Magic is rare: the potion, the prophecy and the Pool of Knowledge. Everything else has a garden explanation.
- Companion lines are one breath on the move. Camp scenes may run longer, paced by the player.
- Realism: fix only what an ordinary player would notice, and never let realism make the game less fun.

## 5. Cast

### Amodu
- **Who:** a boy mocked at school for how he looks, who hides at the far end of his own back garden. Shrunk to 5 mm (about ant size) with a full-size boy's strength. The only playable character, apart from short scenes as Opigo (the cold open and the Oyibo flashback).
- **Wants:** to be big again (all game; the Queen can do it after the war). Then to belong (L2). Then to protect the colony (L3).
- **Fears:** being laughed at, and being seen at all. Deeper down, that the mockers are right.
- **Voice:** awkward, honest, brave in bursts, funny about himself. He knows human things and nothing about ant things.
  - *"I'm standing right here, you know."*
  - *"That's my school bag. I just climbed my own school bag."*
  - *"I'm not a thing."*
- **Arc:** hiding → following → standing up → (later) leading.
- **Knows:** L1: ants talk and want a hero; the Queen might fix him; of Akpuru, only what he sees. The ants learn his name when Mum shouts it (M6). L2: the prophecy (the carved wall) and Oyibo's story (Camp 2). L3: the kingdom, the war, and the Queen's answer (after the war). He doesn't know about the gear Opumie carries until the end of L3.

### Opigo
- **Who:** a veteran ant warrior, one of the three musketeers. Brash, proud, blunt; once celebrated, now an "old-timer". His wife left him for Karubo, who went over to the termites (a later thread; **never mentioned in L1–3**).
- **Wants:** his glory back, and the war ended in his lifetime.
- **Fears:** being a has-been; losing another friend as he lost Oyibo.
- **Voice:** short, gruff, clipped; insults as affection. **No "kid" until L2.**
  - *"No. No, no, no. That's a mistake."*
  - *"It's an axe. The sharp end goes at them."*
  - *"We brought him. He's our problem."*
- **Arc:** "mistake" (L1) → cut free from the web by the mistake (L1 M7) → stays out at the gate with him (L1 M8) → "Get some sleep, kid" at the knot-hole (L2 Camp 3) → fights beside the colony's champion (L3). Later: Karubo, and his honour.
- **Combat role:** charges and knocks enemies off balance; draws attention.
- **Knows:** the garden and the old roads by scent. He saw Oyibo bring the cave down. He doesn't know Opumie has Oyibo's gear.

### Opumie
- **Who:** the second musketeer, a sarcastic, lazy-looking strategist. **He.** He pretends not to care and cares most.
- **Wants:** the war won with brains, not bodies; the kid given a chance.
- **Fears:** that Oyibo died for nothing; that he's too old to matter.
- **Voice:** dry, warm, funny; he believes in the kid first. The running gag is his ex-wife: **at most one joke per level**, the first at L2 Camp 1, and the payoff at L3 M2 (she's the colony smith).
  - *"On his own. You were saying?"*
  - *"A mistake who keeps getting back up."*
  - *"A giant can't save us. A giant just steps on us. It has to be one of us, standing up."*
- **Arc:** a believer from the start. His private arc is Oyibo's gear: he pockets the shield in the web, says nothing through Level 2, and lays it all out at the end of Level 3.
- **Combat role:** spots weak points, chews through silk and fibre, and leads the way by scent (on the road and in the dark).
- **Knows:** the most. He knows the gear is Oyibo's the moment he sees it.

### Oyibo
- **Who:** the third musketeer, calm and honourable, the peacemaker. In Ugo's cave he brought the roof down on himself so the others could escape.
- **Voice (flashback only; new lines, not the draft's):** *"Go. The story's bigger than me."* / *"Legends change. Some of them end."*
- **Later (from the user's story, timing OPEN):** he survived by drinking from the Pool of Knowledge, learned he is the son of the termite priest who made the prophecy, and became "Him". **Not revealed in L1–3.** It's hinted only by his gear, the termite scent on his helmet, and his name scratched out at the Old Camp.

### Queen Titania
- **Who:** the ant queen. She believes the prophecy when others don't, but doubts *this* boy.
- **Wants:** her colony to survive.
- **Voice:** formal, measured. *"This is the saviour? He can barely stand."*
- **Arc:** doubt (he trips bowing) → the trials → names him champion → calls up reinforcements against Akpuru (L3). She **can** make him big again, **after the war**: she has the means, but the potion can't be spared or risked while the colony is at war. (OPEN: the exact reason.)
- **Knows:** the prophecy's origin, and the potion's.

### General Mba
- **Who:** the colony's scarred general. He wants warriors, not myths.
- **Arc:** scoffs at the saviour; is the champion Amodu spars in the **L3 trials**; loses, and is the first to salute.
- **Voice:** *"Myths don't hold gates. Show me."*

### The colony smith
- **Who:** the master smith who upgrades the axe (L3). **Opumie's ex-wife.** She's lovely to Amodu and terrifying to Opumie, and the payoff of the ex-wife gag.
- **Voice:** *"You still owe me a spear, Opumie."*

### Akpuru
- **Who:** the termite general. He hunts the trio from Level 1.
- **Wants:** to crush the ants' hope before it grows.
- **Voice:** few words, contempt. Through the stone at Root Hall: *"Hide in your hole, little giant. I'll be here when you crawl out."*
- **Arc:** hears of Amodu from a raider who runs (L1 M3) → can't be beaten, only held off at the gate (L1 M8) → his tunnellers follow them into the tree (L2) → stuck in the resin pit (L3 M5).
- **The mercy choice (two light branches):** **mercy** is hauling him out with a rope from the pit's rim (step in and you'd stick too). He goes, and later turns up to help at the Citadel. **The other choice** hands him to the colony's soldiers; he escapes, bitter, and Karubo leads the hunt. **The branches meet again at the Citadel.** Nobody dies by Amodu's hand.

### Gate guards
Loyal, frightened, by the book. *"Halt! The gate is sealed."* / *"You two can come in. Not that thing."* / *"Termites! From the east!"* (PENDING: the route round the pond)

### Others in Levels 1–3
- **The bully:** tall and confident. Only his legs and trainers are ever shown. **Nameless.** His soda can causes the mix-up.
- **Mum:** never seen above the knees. Her search and watering can (L1), the kitchen light (L2), and her message on **Amodu's own phone**, dropped from his bag in M1 (hidden mission).
- **The con beetle:** an old beetle who conned the musketeers with a fake map and spoiled honey. He's at the Capstone's bead shrine selling "holy dew"; catch him for their food back and a map fragment marking the hidden missions.
- **The weaver captain:** polite, disciplined; the first of another colony to respect Amodu (L2).
- **Ugo:** a blind, mad shrew who drank from the Pool of Knowledge. He speaks in riddles, is dangerous, and his "children" are spiders. Seen in the L2 flashback. Not the end of the story.
- **Creatures (not characters):** termite raiders; the orb-weaver (L1); a ground beetle bigger than a car (L1); grubs and the Maw (L2); the diving beetle (L3).

### Later (named, not in L1–3)
Zina the dragonfly (flight comes with her; she's a reformed ant-eater), Karubo, Queen Termina, Silkfang, the Celestial Fangs (Ruknash, Nillix, Krothak), Akpombo, King Craxus.

## 6. The world

**Scale:** ×360. 1 m in game = 2.8 mm real. Amodu is 1.8 m in game (5 mm real), about the same size as the ants. A 1 cm pebble is 3.6 m; the pencil is a 68 m log; his school bag is a 150 m mountain. Sizes and positions: `world-facts.md`.

**Where:** an ordinary family back garden in a **warm country that's never named**. The house, with the patio and back door, is south; the apple tree is west; a lawn, a flower bed, and a vegetable patch at the north end; a wooden fence round it all. It's summer, and Level 1 starts on a clear morning after overnight rain.

**The playable area** is the ±360 m square of lawn. **The house, the shed and the compost heap are scenery outside it** for now. Reaching the Citadel and Ugo's cave later needs the map extended (a map proposal is owed).

**Under the garden:** the Ant Kingdom, a city of fungus-light and amber, with its gate on the Bare Patch. The old way in runs: Root Hall (half-collapsed tunnels; doors he holds open while the ants pass) → the grub nest → the Heartwood Stair inside the trunk → the knot-hole (60 m) → the canopy (the weavers) → the dry way down the far side → the Rootway → the Maw's crossing → the kingdom from underneath.

**The termite side: the south-east.** Their trail comes over the patio edge from the woodpile, with a forward camp south of the Rut. Termites are **dusk raiders**: nearly blind, they avoid daylight, travel under mud tubes, find you by vibration and scent, and **march at dusk**. They build with carton (chewed wood and spit); the L3 causeway is carton laid on floating grass, built at night.

## 7. Lore

- **The prophecy:** long ago, a termite priest who went over to the ants foretold a saviour from the human world who would *stand tall where others crawl* and unite the insect kingdoms; the prophecy's own words call him flawed and unremarkable. It's **carved** (not painted) into Root Hall's wall: a two-legged figure among crawling ones. The ants have read it by touch all their lives. When Amodu's lamp reaches the end, he's the first to *see* a **second carved figure**, a crawling one standing up beside the first. One line from Opigo, no explanation yet. (The ants picked the confident bully against the prophecy's own words; that pays off here.)
- **The war:** the termite empire has swallowed colony after colony. The ant kingdom under the garden is one of the last free ones. Akpuru leads in the field; Queen Termina rules (later).
- **The musketeers:** Opigo, Opumie and Oyibo, the Queen's best in their day, now old-timers. She sent them to find the saviour.
- **Ugo and the cave:** Ugo drank from the Pool of Knowledge and went mad; he knows everyone's secrets. His cave is under the shed. The musketeers went there to learn where the saviour was. They answered three riddles (**a hole, a coin, an echo**), fought his spider children, and Oyibo brought the roof down so the other two could escape. Ugo's parting taunt to Oyibo, about running from his own story, is the first seed of "Him" (written fresh, not quoted).
- **How the ants reached the boxes:** the collapse broke into the water butt's overflow. **Mum was watering**, so the water was running, and they were washed down the hose (the Endless Snake) and spat out of the leaking coupling onto the lawn.
- **The riddles become verbs** (L2): dig, flip, shout. The shout finds the way through the dark Rootway; digging opens the pit the Maw falls into.
- **The potion:** the colony's one dose, brewed long ago from the Pool's water, which the defecting termite priest brought (OPEN: confirm). It only works on giants. The soda made its mist. The Queen can reverse it, after the war.
- **Oyibo's gear:** the **shield** with a musketeer's mark (L1, the orb web); the **helmet, smelling of termites** (L2, the Maw's lair); a **musketeer's mark pressed into the causeway mud** (L3). Plus the **Musketeers' Old Camp** in the canopy (L2 hidden): three names in the bark, with Oyibo's scratched out, from the inside, recently.

8. **Level 2 (that night and the next day):** the Root Hall camp (the fire lamp; crafting begins); the carved wall; the grubs; the tale of Oyibo, played; the Heartwood Stair; the kitchen light and "kid" on the ledge; the weavers' prison and the silk rope; Apple Fall; the Rootway, with the shout and Akpuru's tunnellers behind; the Maw and the helmet; the kingdom seen from below.
9. **Level 3 (three nights):** in through the old door ("Which you sealed"); the whispers; the trials (the resin boulder; sparring Mba), the smith, champion; the Queen: after the war; the causeway grows each night, so scout it, raft, the diving beetle, break it; the war plan; Akpuru in the resin pit, mercy or the soldiers; the celebration; Opumie lays out the shield, the helmet and the causeway mark: *"…so how are his things getting out?"*
