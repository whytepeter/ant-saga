## Premise and structure
- **The trio adventure** (2026-09-26): two old warrior ants, Opigo and Opumie, shrink Amodu so he'll help end the insect war; they travel with him as companions. Replaces SMALL GIANT's "get home before sunset".
- **Why they shrink him:** the saviour must *stand tall where others crawl*; a giant just steps on them. Opumie: *"A giant can't save us. A giant just steps on us. It has to be one of us, standing up."*
- **Amodu's goal:** to be big again. *"Can you make me big again?"* / *"Not us. The Queen might."* Helping end the war comes later, as he belongs.
- **The game runs to about Level 10.** Don't plan or fix the ending yet. **Ugo is not the end of the story.**
- Levels: 1 the backyard (ends shut out, into Root Hall); 2 the apple tree (roots, trunk, canopy, the Maw); 3 the kingdom and the pond.
- Setting: an ordinary back garden; the kingdom is under it. (The Nigerian compound was dropped.)
- Content is family-friendly: no sexual content, innuendo or seduction.

## Characters and names
- **Amodu is about the same size as the ants.** Size gives neither side an edge (not smaller, no squeezing through gaps). He's not heavy enough to break ant floors. His edge is a full-size boy's strength.
- Opumie is **he**. The dragonfly is **Zina** (not Zara). **Oyibo** keeps his name.
- Opigo and Opumie are companions with short subtitled lines; no voice acting yet.
- **Keep simple beats simple.** Props don't need backstory.

## Weapons and gear
- The ants give Amodu an **ordinary ant axe** at M3 (The Cut Road) when he first has to fight. Nothing is "too heavy for any ant"; weapons are normal-sized.
- The **knife** is given at the **First Night** camp as an everyday tool; at M7 (the orb web) he uses it.
- **Armour:** one piece per story event (leaf wraps, beetle-shell plates, a champion's mantle), no light/balanced/heavy tiers.

## Survival and building

- **Crafting starts in Level 2.** Level 1 has only fire, shelter, food and water.
- The build system is data-driven (weapons, lamps, shelters, camps, defences). Every recipe exists to get the player further.

## The survival design, decided 2026-09-27 (`docs/SURVIVAL.md`)
- **Goal:** find a way to be big again, as a light story found in the world, plus bosses across the areas and a real ending (then keep playing small).
- **Great beasts are puzzles, in any order:** the orb weaver (high, the canopy), the Maw (deep, the Rootway), the wolf spider (far, across the Rut); each guards one ingredient of the Queen's growth draught. Then the termite Citadel ends the war and the game.
- **The termite front moves:** mud tubes spread across the garden over the days, eat wood, raid camps; push them back.
- **Death:** drop your pack where you fell, wake at your bed, go back for it.
- **Start:** with a stone knife (found beside him with the vial) to gather materials; the stone axe is the first craft.
- **Amodu's thoughts:** short, rare subtitles at discoveries and first-time moments.
- Not taken: "the humans are the weather" (mower, watering, sprinkler events) and "growing is the progression".
- The build order in SURVIVAL.md §7 replaces the world order below (the kingdom becomes the Act 1 hub).

## Work order, decided 2026-09-27
- **Survival before story, inside the story mode** (not a separate sandbox). Story beats (cold open, First Night, web rescue, beetle, the other Meshy models) wait.

## Accepted 2026-09-27 from an outside review
- **Vertical slice first (N125):** build the cold open through the First Night camp until it feels finished and surprising, before widening to the rest of Level 1 and beyond.
- **Hunger never lowers his strength (N126):** keep both meters (hunger and thirst), tuned gentle; running low slows stamina, and an empty meter only drains health slowly. The heave limits stay fixed: full human strength is the core rule.
- **Set up the coin before the gate (N127):** no "examine" system. The coin appears earlier on the route, where the player sees it roll (or hears the ants call it the Brass Moon), so the gate fight pays it off.
- **The shout has a cost (N128):** in the Rootway, each shout finds the way by its echo but also draws Akpuru's tunnellers closer.
- **One scripted shower in Level 1 (N129):** a short rain on day 2 that forces the trio to take cover (the rain system already exists in `world/weather.gd`).
- **Old world bible archived (N130):** `docs/WORLD.md` moved to `docs/archive/WORLD.md`; the current world is `docs/narrative/world-facts.md`.
- Not taken from the review: a single "vitality" meter, a separate companion AI plan (the companions already follow, lead and regroup), trimming Level 1 lore (the bible already limits it to three facts).
