Layers

The plan

  • L1 unchanged in shape; First Night moves 80 m south so the route stops doubling back.
  • L1 raiders march along the top of the driveway kerb, then drop to the lawn north of the pond: visible from the Capstone, no new geometry.
  • L2: the tree grounds get a loop that works: canopy, the weavers' silk ladder down the west face, the Leaf-Litter Moor (blackbird), the West Root Door into the Rootway.
  • L3: the kingdom under the Bare Patch; the war plan on the north shore; the raft rides the hose runoff into the pond; the Silent Post is a raft side trip.
  • The south opens in L4 across the captured causeway (and the Maw's hole from the Rootway): the Frontier, using everything already built there.
  • L4 ends at Spider's Edge: the wolf spider guards the trowel, the only way up onto the patio.
  • L5 crosses the patio slabs to the woodpile Citadel; the back door is a side beat, not the goal.
  • Later: follow the hose upstream to the water butt and the shed (Ugo's cave); the compost heap becomes a rot-and-fungus place, not the termites' side.
  • Removed: the lolly stick, the Beetle Stump, the outdoor Moss Wall and Glow-Fungus Ring pins.
